## Project creation

- Keep the project's status up to date.
- Attach the relevant files to the project.
- Create the appropriate milestones.
- Set the dates of the project and its milestones.
- Assign the project to me.

## Issues

- A description of 2 to 3 lines, then about 4 to 5 checkboxes.
- Progress goes in one relevant comment under the issue, updated in place rather than added anew.
- The issue links its newest PR, which replaces the older link.
- Attach the relevant files to the issue.
- Keep the issue's status up to date with the work.
- Tick a checkbox once the progress comment addresses it, even if the work isn't complete.
